What to email about

You’re an author who wants a feature day

Tell me your book, your genre, and the date you have in mind, especially if it’s a launch week. The authors page has the pricing and how a feature day works. Subject line Booky feature day gets you to the front.

You’re a reader with a book to suggest

Send it. Booky, the daily romantasy word game, only works because the books in it are ones readers actually vouch for, and a good chunk of the queue came from suggestions exactly like yours.

Something is broken

Today’s word won’t accept a real word, your streak vanished, the page looks wrong on your phone. Tell me what device and browser you’re on and I’ll get on it. Bug reports are genuinely welcome, not a nuisance.

You’re press, or you run a site or newsletter

Happy to help with anything you need: screenshots, logos, the story of how it got built, or an interview. If you run a game directory or a book newsletter and want to list Booky, the daily romantasy word game, just say so and I’ll send over whatever format you need.

Your email or your data

Want off the reader newsletter, or want to know what I hold on you and have it deleted? Ask and it’s done. Every email also has a one-click unsubscribe link. The privacy page spells out what gets collected and why.

Replying to a Booky email works too

You don’t have to copy the address out. Hitting reply on any Booky email reaches the same inbox, because every one of them is set to reply back to hello@90books.com.
