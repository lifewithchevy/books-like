Booky

How to play

Guess the Booky in 6 tries.

  • Each guess must be a 5-letter word.
  • The tile colors will change to show how close your guess was.

Today's word is romantasy-coded. Think fae, dragons, fated mates, sharp blades, the usual.

Examples

FATED

F is in the word and in the right spot.

COURT

O is in the word but in the wrong spot.

BLADE

D is not in the word.

A new word unlocks at midnight UTC. Don't break your streak.
