About 90books

Made by one person, Olga Roman, since March 2026.

Hi, I’m Olga. I read a lot of romantasy, I kept running out of things to read next, and the answer was always the same: ask a stranger on Reddit. So I built the thing I wanted instead.

What’s here

Booky, the daily romantasy word game. One five-letter word a day. Guess it and you unlock a book, with the cover, the author, and why it’s worth your time. Free, no account.

Book recommendations. Tell the site a book you loved and it gives you the next one, sorted by mood and genre. Every book Booky has featured is on the Booky library page.

How a book gets chosen

No algorithm picks these. I read the genre myself and I handpick every word and the book it’s tied to.

  • The word has to genuinely belong to the book, not just have five letters.
  • I only feature books I’d hand to a friend.
  • Paying for a feature day never buys a good review. An author can buy the date their book appears. They can’t buy what I say about it.

How it’s paid for

Authors can book a feature day, and some book links are Amazon affiliate links that may earn me a small cut at no cost to you. No ads, no ad trackers, and I don’t sell reader data. The privacy page is the long version.
