Good to know

Romantasy book promotion FAQ

How much does book promotion on Booky cost?

$79 for one feature day, or $199 for a 3-day launch pack you can spread across release week. That’s the whole price. No setup fee, no cut of your sales, and I won’t come back at you with an upsell.

What kinds of books do you accept?

Romantasy, and romance with a good fantasy thread running through it. Booky’s readers turn up for that one genre, so a book outside it won’t get featured no matter what it pays. I check every book within 7 business days and refund you in full if it isn’t a fit.

How many readers will see my book?

About 120 romantasy readers start a game every day, and everyone who finishes sees your cover, your name and your Amazon link. That includes the ones who don’t get it, because the book is revealed either way. Your book is the answer to the puzzle, so it isn’t something anyone scrolls past.

How is this different from a book promotion newsletter?

A newsletter blast asks readers to skim an email. Booky asks them to play, and your book is the reveal at the end of the puzzle they just solved. You also keep the shareable graphic, a permanent spot on the Booky Library shelf, and a mention in the reader newsletter.

When do I get the graphic, and what do I do with it?

Once your book is approved I email you the finished card, at least three days before your feature day. Nothing to design and nothing to brief: post it to Stories, Reels or TikTok, send it to your newsletter or your street team. The link is printed on the card, so people can get to the game even from a screenshot.

Can you guarantee sales?

No, and I’d be careful with anyone in book promo who says they can. What you get is the feature, the graphic, and an honest report of how many readers played and how many clicked through.
