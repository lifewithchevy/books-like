Your book, the word of the day

Booky is the daily romantasy word game by 90books. Every day, readers solve a 5-letter word hidden in a romantasy book. When they win, the book is revealed: cover, author, and a buy link. For one day, that book can be yours.

▶ See it live: play today’s word

How it works

  1. Say hi. DM me on Instagram (@lifewith_chevy) or email me. Tell me your book. Bonus points: suggest the 5-letter word that sums it up (a real, guessable word so players can actually win it).
  2. Book your day. If your book fits Booky’s vibe, I confirm your word and date and send a payment link. $25 founder price for my first five authors.
  3. Your feature day. Readers play your word all day and meet your book on the win screen. Afterwards you get an honest report: how many readers played, how many clicked through to your book.

What you get

  • Your cover and a “Get it on Amazon” link on the win screen, all day
  • A custom shareable graphic for your Instagram and TikTok
  • A permanent spot on the Booky Library shelf
  • A mention in that week’s reader newsletter
  • The honest report, real numbers, screenshots included

The honest part

I can’t promise sales. Nobody in book promo honestly can. What I promise is the feature, the assets, and a real report of what happened. And because readers trust Booky only as long as every book genuinely fits, I do say no when a book isn’t the right match. Paid features are always labeled in the game.

A small but mighty audience

Booky is new and growing. A few dozen romantasy readers play every single day, and every one of them sees the day’s book. No bots, no vanity numbers, just the exact readers you write for.
