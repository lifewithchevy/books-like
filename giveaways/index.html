How it works

How a Booky giveaway works

A normal book giveaway, except that on two of the fourteen days your book is the answer to the puzzle. Players solve a word out of it before they ever see the cover.

1

Book your month and pay

The checkout asks for your book, the month and the format. Book at least three weeks out. If you have a release date, the run closes a few days before it, the way publishers schedule giveaways elsewhere.

2

I check it fits, usually within two or three days

I pick the two words your book will hide and confirm your dates. If it is not right for my readers, you get a full refund.

3

You send the copies and pay $39

Three to ten copies, however you normally deliver them: BookFunnel, a Kindle gift, an Apple or Kobo code, or the file itself. No print, so no postage and no addresses.

4

It runs for fourteen days

Your cover and buy link sit on the win screen the whole time. On two days, at least five days apart, the answer comes out of your book. Those two days then stay in the Booky archive, where players keep solving them long after the giveaway ends.

5

I draw the winner, you send the book

I draw within two days and send you the winner’s email. You send the book within four weeks. Then you get the report: plays, finishers, entrants, clicks, and the archive plays that keep coming in.

The graphic

You post this, your people play your book

Once your book is approved, I email you a finished card, sized for Stories and TikTok, at least three days before your giveaway opens so you have time to schedule it. Your cover, your title, your name, and the link printed on the graphic itself, so it still works when someone screenshots it or reshares it without the sticker. You don’t design anything and you don’t brief anyone.

The link is printed on the card, not just attached as a story sticker, so it survives a screenshot and a reshare.

Good to know

Book giveaway FAQ

How much does a book giveaway on Booky cost?

$39 for a fourteen-day giveaway, paid at the checkout. That is the whole price. No setup fee, no cut of your sales, no upsell. You supply the copies, which for an ebook costs you nothing.

Who sends the book to the winner?

You do, and that is how book giveaways work everywhere: the host is solely responsible for getting the prize to the winner. I draw the winner and send you their email address, and you send the book. I never hold your file or stand between you and the reader.

Can I give away print copies?

Not here. Ebook and audiobook only, so there are no addresses, no postage and no customs for either of us. Three to ten copies is the usual range, and on a digital file extra copies cost you nothing.

Do I get the email addresses of everyone who enters?

No, only the winner’s, and only so you can send the book. That is the standard everywhere and I hold it too. You also must not add the winner to a mailing list or ask them for a review, and I will not book you again if you do. My readers gave me their email for a word game, not to be sold to.

When do I need to send the prize?

Within four weeks of the giveaway closing. The winner has a week to claim, and if they go quiet I draw again and tell you. If something goes wrong on your side, tell me before the four weeks is up rather than after.

What kinds of books do you accept?

Romantasy, and romance with a good fantasy thread running through it. Booky’s readers turn up for that one genre, so a book outside it will not run no matter what it pays. I check every book myself, usually within two or three days, and refund you in full if it is not right. I reserve the right to refuse a book for any reason, which is what keeps the shelf something readers trust.

How many readers will see my book?

Enough that the last giveaway took over 100 entrants, and every one of them reads romantasy. I will give you the current numbers when you book, because they move month to month. What does not move is who they are: a genre audience rather than a general one, so your book is in front of people who already read what you write.

How is this different from a big book giveaway site?

Most people run both, and at $39 this is meant to sit on top of what you already book rather than replace it. The difference is the room: on a big site your giveaway sits in a list of hundreds shown to everyone who reads anything, while here it is the only book given away that month, in front of romantasy readers only, and on two of the days the answer to the puzzle comes out of your book.

Can I run a giveaway if I’m self published?

That is most of who books it. Indie and small press authors are the whole point, and your book sits on the same shelf as the traditionally published ones. I don’t care who published it, only whether my readers will want it.

When do I get the graphic, and what do I do with it?

Once your book is approved I email you the finished card, at least three days before your giveaway opens. Nothing to design and nothing to brief: post it to Stories, Reels or TikTok, send it to your newsletter or your street team. The link is printed on the card, so people can get to the game even from a screenshot.

How far ahead do I need to book?

At least three weeks. That covers checking your book, picking the word and getting your graphic to you a few days before you post it. Only one giveaway runs a month, so release weeks fill up first. If your release is sooner, email me anyway and I will tell you honestly whether I can fit you in.

What if I change my mind, or my release slips?

Tell me before it goes live and you get a full refund or a later month, whichever you prefer. Once it is live I cannot refund it, because the word is locked into the queue, your graphic is made and your book has its shelf page.

Can I give away an advance reader copy?

Yes, and ARC readers are a good fit here because the people playing are already looking for their next romantasy read. Say it is an ARC when you book and I will label it as one on the giveaway, so nobody is expecting a finished copy.

What happens to my book after the giveaway ends?

Your two days stay in the Booky archive, where players work through old puzzles long after the day has passed. Your cover and buy link are still on them. Those archive plays are counted and they go in your report, so the number usually keeps climbing after the giveaway closes.

Who are Booky’s readers and where do they come from?

Romantasy readers, mostly from the subreddits where the genre actually lives. They turn up from a thread about ACOTAR or Fourth Wing, play a word game about the books they already read, and keep coming back daily. Nobody is here by accident and nobody is here for a different genre, which is the only reason a small audience is worth paying for.

Can you guarantee sales or reviews?

No, and I would be careful with anyone in book promo who says they can. You also cannot ask the winner for a review, here or anywhere else that runs giveaways properly. What you get is the giveaway, the two feature days, the graphic, and an honest report of how many played, finished, entered and clicked.
